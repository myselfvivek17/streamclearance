How StreamClearance handles your video

In plain language: what we keep, for how long, and where it is processed.

What we collect

  • The video you upload, its file name, size and length.
  • The countries you choose.
  • The report: what the video shows and says (transcript and on-screen text), the moments flagged and why.
  • Standard web request logs kept by Google Cloud (address requested, IP address, time). They carry report ids, not your content.

We only use this to run the check you asked for. No account, no tracking, no advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Your video is deleted as soon as its check ends, whether it finished, failed or was cancelled. A video you upload but never check is deleted after {{unused_ttl}}.
  • Reports are deleted {{report_ttl}} after they are created. You can delete a report at any time with “Delete this report now”; it is removed straight away.
  • Request logs are kept by Google Cloud Logging for 30 days.
  • Your browser keeps its own copy of the video you selected (to play it next to the report) until you close or reload the page.

Where it is processed

  • The app and its storage: {{app_where}}.
  • The video and its transcript are analysed by {{gemini_where}}

Who else is involved

One sub-processor: Google Cloud (Cloud Run hosting, Cloud Storage for large videos during a check, Gemini on Vertex AI, Vertex AI RAG Engine for searching the law texts, Cloud Logging).

Your choices

Principles

StreamClearance is designed with Malaysia PDPA 2010 and India DPDP Act 2023 principles in mind (data minimisation, purpose limitation, retention limits). This page describes how the app works; it is not a statement of legal compliance.

The results are an automated pre-check, not legal advice. A person makes the publish decision.
